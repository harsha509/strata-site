Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026.

Graphwright does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. This page explains exactly what the app does with information on your device.

Data you create

Graphs, node text, edge labels, and ink strokes are stored only on your iPad, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded by the app. If iCloud backup is enabled on your device, Apple may include the app's data in your device backup under Apple's own privacy terms.

Exports

When you use Copy or Share, the app places a text bundle on the clipboard or hands it to the iPadOS share sheet. What happens next is controlled by you and by the app you send it to. Graphwright does not see or record where an export goes.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • No network requests of any kind.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.

Children

Graphwright is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that sends data off the device, for example calling a language model from inside the app, this policy will be updated before that version ships and the effective date above will change.
