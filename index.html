Graphwright

Design graph-engineering workflows on iPad and hand them to an LLM.

Graphwright is a canvas for the graphs behind agent systems: goal decompositions, workflows, agent capabilities, team structures, and communication patterns. Place boxes, connect them with typed arrows, and attach context to every node. When the design is ready, export one text bundle that any language model can read.

What it does

  • Five graph layers with the node and edge vocabulary from Graph Engineering in the Era of LLM Agents (arXiv 2608.21156).
  • A plain white sheet with a faint grid and rulers, drag to place, drag to connect.
  • A details panel for each node: what it does, full context, inputs, outputs, constraints, tags.
  • Apple Pencil ink for annotations.
  • Export as Mermaid, JSON, and per-node cards in a single bundle.

Privacy in one line

Your graphs live on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account so they follow you across devices. No developer accounts, no analytics, no servers of ours. Read the privacy policy.
