Common questions

Where are my graphs stored?

On your iPad only. Deleting the app deletes its graphs. Export a bundle first if you want to keep a copy elsewhere.

How do I send a graph to an AI?

Open the graph, tap the share icon at the top right, then Copy or Share. Paste the bundle into any chat with a language model.

How do I add a branch?

Connect the deciding node to each outcome with a logical edge and put the rule in the edge's Condition field.

Can I use Apple Pencil?

Yes. Switch the toolbar to Ink to draw over the sheet. Ink is saved with the graph but is not part of the export.
